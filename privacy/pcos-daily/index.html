Privacy Policy

PCOS Daily

A privacy-first PCOS tracking app. No accounts, no servers, no tracking.

Effective: May 19, 2026

PCOS Daily does not collect, transmit, or store any of your data on external servers. Everything stays on your device.

What We Collect

Nothing. We do not operate servers that receive or store your data. All information you enter — symptoms, cycle events, medications, and notes — lives in a local database on your iPhone, encrypted at rest by iOS.

What We Share

Nothing. PCOS Daily has no advertising partners, no analytics services, and no third-party SDKs that receive your health data. The app makes zero network requests.

Third-Party Services

Version 1.0 of PCOS Daily includes no third-party code. No analytics, no crash reporting SDKs, no ad networks. If future versions add optional services, this policy will be updated and you will be notified in-app before any data leaves your device.

iCloud Sync

If you enable iCloud on your device, your data may sync through Apple’s iCloud infrastructure using end-to-end encryption. Elite Zone Apex never has access to this data. You can disable iCloud sync at any time in your device’s Settings.

Crash Reports

If you opt in through Apple’s standard diagnostics settings, Apple may send anonymous crash reports to us via App Store Connect. These reports contain technical information about the crash and never include your health data, symptoms, or personal information.

Your Data Rights

You own your data completely. You can export all of it at any time (Settings > Export) as a JSON file. You can permanently delete all data at any time (Settings > Data > Delete All). Deletion is immediate and irreversible — because we have no servers, there are no backups on our end to worry about.

Children’s Privacy

PCOS Daily is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children. Since the app collects no data at all, no special provisions are necessary.

Medical Disclaimer

PCOS Daily is a personal tracking tool. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always consult a qualified healthcare provider about your health.

Changes to This Policy

If we update this policy, the revised version will be posted here with a new effective date. Material changes — especially any that involve data collection — will be communicated through an in-app notice before taking effect.
